Pack and plan

TravelMetro runs late during Puja and usually beats cars once diversions start. Use app cabs only for short hops.
CarryComfortable shoes, water, a power bank, a light raincoat, and a small bag. Large bags may be refused.
EatRolls, phuchka and mughlai paratha. Community bhog is served on Ashtami and Navami at many pujas.
CheckKolkata Police traffic advisories and local news for one-way pandal lanes and opening times.
Pandal list and route order follow KolkataKhoj (kolkatakhoj.com/pandals), read on 10 October 2026. Their list says Dakshinpara, Arjunpur Amra Sabai Club and Azadgarh Sebak Sangha may not be held in 2026. Check before you go. Ashtami and Navami dates differ by a day between panchangs. Any cafe or eatery listings are not paid placements unless marked, and prices are approximate. Map buttons open a Google Maps search, and because pandals are temporary, the pin can be approximate.
